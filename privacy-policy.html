Privacy Policy

Context Cards · Last updated 25 October 2026

Context Cards doesn't collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no ads.

Your words and cards

Everything you add (words, notes, cards, tags and photos) is saved only on your iPhone. It isn't sent to us or to anyone else. It's included in your iPhone's own backups, and it's deleted when you delete the app.

Photos

When you add a photo to a card, you choose it with Apple's photo picker. The app only receives the photo you pick, not your photo library, and keeps it on your iPhone.

Song search

When you search for a song, the words you type are sent to Apple's iTunes Search service to find matching songs, covers and previews. This goes straight from your iPhone to Apple; we never see it. Apple's handling of it is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
