Common questions

How do I add a word?
Tap ··· at the top right of the daily card or Home, type the word, and tap Add word.
How do I add a card to a word?
Open the word, tap ···, then New card. Choose Text, Image or Audio, fill it in, and tap Add card.
How do I write my thoughts on a card?
Tap a card to flip it. Press and hold it to edit the card, including that back side.
How do I delete words or cards?
Tap ··· and choose Delete word (on Home or the daily card) or Delete card (on a word's page). Tap the ones to remove, then the trash button.
Do I need an account?
No. Everything is saved on your iPhone, with no sign-in.
Will my words move to a new iPhone?
Yes, if you restore your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one. Your words aren't synced between devices.
Why can't I find lyrics for my song?
Apple's song search gives the title, artist, cover and a preview, but not lyrics. Type the lines that use your word yourself.
